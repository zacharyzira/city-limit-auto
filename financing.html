Financing

We work with fleet lenders who understand trailers — not just trucks. Get a straight answer, fast.

How it works

Whether you're picking up one trailer or building out a fleet, we help structure financing that fits how you actually use your equipment — not a one-size-fits-all lease.

Submit the form and we'll follow up within one business day with rate ranges based on your credit profile, down payment, and term. No obligation, no hard pull until you're ready to move forward.

We also work directly with owner-operators and small fleets who may not fit traditional bank underwriting — ask us about alternative options if a standard loan doesn't fit.

What you'll need

  • Business or personal ID
  • Proof of income / business revenue
  • Down payment (varies by term)
  • MC/DOT number, if applicable
