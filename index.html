No Extra Charge

We handle the DMV paperwork

Buy a trailer from us and you drive off with plates and registration in hand. We register the unit, pull the paperwork, and hand you the plate — you don't make a DMV appointment or stand in a line.

That includes the VIN verification, which is its own headache on a trailer. We take care of it here.

None of this costs extra. It's built into the price of the trailer, not tacked on at signing.

What's included

  • Title & registration filed for you
  • License plates before you leave
  • VIN verification done on site
  • No doc fees or add-ons
