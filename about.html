About City Limit Auto

A licensed California trailer dealership built on straight talk and clean paperwork.

Our story

City Limit Auto is a California-licensed trailer dealer specializing in dry van trailers for small and local businesses. Based in Perris, California, we provide dependable commercial trailers, transparent pricing, and a straightforward buying process.

Our focus is helping local operators, contractors, and growing fleets find reliable dry van trailers they can trust — without pressure or unnecessary complexity.

We also run a full trailer repair operation with our own team of mechanics. That's how every unit on our lot gets inspected before it's listed — and it means we can keep your equipment running after the sale, whether you bought from us or not.

As a licensed dealer we handle the DMV side in-house: registration, plates, and VIN verification are done right here, so you're not making a DMV appointment or standing in a line. Sales tax, DMV fees, and our doc fee apply and are due at purchase.
