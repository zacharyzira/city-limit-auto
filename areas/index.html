Areas We Serve

Used dry van trailers for truckers across the Inland Empire & Los Angeles

Customers drive to our Perris yard from every city in the Inland Empire and Los Angeles. Where you are coming from does not change what you need, and we keep the yard stocked for it.

What They Want

The same trailer, wherever you are coming from

The city a customer comes from does not tell us what they will buy. Truckers here haul for many different customers, and what they need changes from one load to the next.

Most of them still want the same thing: a 53-foot dry van with swing doors, and very often a food grade unit. Nearly all of our inventory has swing doors, and we always have food grade trailers available, along with both spring ride and air ride suspension.

What most people ask for

  • 53' dry vans
  • Swing doors on almost every unit
  • Food grade units (plate and plastic)
  • Spring ride or air ride
  • A range of makes, years and prices
Why They Make the Drive

One yard for the whole job

Most of our trailers are ten years old or older, which keeps them affordable and still gets the job done. We are a licensed dealer, so we register the trailer and handle the paperwork and plates in one stop.

We usually have a large group of trailers available at a time, in different makes, years and price ranges, so you can compare them in one visit. Our own mechanics can repair a unit or install new tires for an additional cost, and we offer financing through our lending partner.
