Serving Fontana, Ontario & Bloomington

Used dry van trailers for Fontana, Ontario & Bloomington truckers

Owner-operators and small fleets from Fontana, Ontario and Bloomington make the drive to our Perris yard every month. Here is what they come for, and why they keep coming back.

Who Shops With Us

Truckers from Fontana, Ontario and Bloomington

A few customers a month come to us from each of Fontana, Ontario and Bloomington. Nearly all of them are owner-operators and small fleet owners running their own trucking companies, and now and then a pallet company.

They haul for several different customers at once, so what goes in the trailer changes all the time. They do not need a trailer built for one job. They need a dependable dry van that can take whatever load comes next.

That is why most of them ask for the same thing: a 53-foot dry van with swing doors. Nearly all of our inventory has swing doors, and we keep both spring ride and air ride suspension in stock.

Customers come to us from the rest of the Inland Empire and Los Angeles County too. See all areas we serve.

What they usually buy

  • 53' dry vans
  • Swing doors on almost every unit
  • Spring ride or air ride
  • Food grade units (plate and plastic)
  • A range of makes, years and prices
Why They Choose Us

Older trailers that still get the job done

Most of our trailers are ten years old or older. That is the appeal: they cost far less than a newer unit and still do the work, which keeps the price of getting started or adding a truck within reach for a small fleet or an owner-operator.

We are a licensed dealer, so you can get everything done in one stop. We register the trailer and handle the paperwork and plates, so you do not have to buy from one seller, register somewhere else and find a mechanic on your own.

You also do not have to drive around comparing. We usually have a large group of trailers available at a time, in different makes, years and price ranges, all in one place. You can look at several in a single visit instead of visiting a handful of different sellers.

If a trailer needs work, we do that ourselves too. Every trailer is inspected before it is listed, and our own mechanics can fix it up or install new tires for an additional cost, so you get to work and start earning sooner.
